The Problem
Africa is not a
marketing problem.
It is a distribution problem.

Products raise millions, run ad campaigns, partner with influencers — and still cannot prove how many real African users downloaded, used, and stayed active. The infrastructure to convert awareness into verified adoption has never existed. Until now.

📊

Impressions ≠ Users

Online ads generate millions of impressions but convert less than 0.01% into real active users. The biggest drop-off is between download and first transaction.

🌍

No Structural Distribution

Street creators work alone. Ambassador programs are unverified. Nobody has built the coordinated, tracked, documented infrastructure that turns Africa into a real acquisition channel.

📋

Zero Proof of Adoption

A protocol flies into Lagos, runs events, flies home. They see impressions and reach on a dashboard. They cannot tell you who is still using the product 30 days later.
